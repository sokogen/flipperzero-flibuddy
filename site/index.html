FliBuddy

A Flipper Zero spool reader for 3D printing filament.

Hold a Bambu Lab spool tag to the back of the Flipper and it shows what the spool is: the material, the color by Bambu's name and code, the diameter, the label weight and the temperatures. No phone and no network are needed for that. NTAG tags show their type, UID and size.

With the FliBuddy app for iPhone and iPad and its Apple Watch companion, the Flipper also keeps your own Bambuddy server up to date: look a spool up, add new spools, record weighings, link a tag to a spool and browse the inventory.

What you need

  • A Flipper Zero on the current official firmware release (1.5.1 at the time of writing).
  • For the phone app: iOS, iPadOS or watchOS 26.
  • For syncing: a Bambuddy server in internal inventory mode, reachable over HTTPS, and an API key with inventory read and update and settings read. Spoolman mode is not supported.
