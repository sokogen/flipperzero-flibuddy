Privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-11-05.

FliBuddy does not collect any data for its developer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no tracking and no ads, and nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party.

What stays on your devices

  • The Bambuddy address and the app settings are stored on the device (in the app's preferences).
  • The Bambuddy API key is stored in the iOS Keychain. On the Apple Watch it is kept in the watch's Keychain.
  • The Flipper app stores its settings in a small file on the Flipper's SD card.
  • The apps keep a local diagnostic log on the device. It is excluded from backups and is not sent anywhere unless you choose Share Log in Settings, which hands it to the share sheet with secrets such as the API key redacted.

Where data goes

Data moves only between your own devices and your own server:

  • between the Flipper and your iPhone, iPad or Apple Watch over Bluetooth;
  • between your iPhone and your Apple Watch over Apple's WatchConnectivity;
  • between the apps and the Bambuddy server whose address you enter, which you run yourself. What that server does with the data is up to you.

Camera

The camera is used only to scan the QR code Bambuddy shows when it creates an API key. Images are not saved or sent.

Contact
