## v1.0

- First release.
- Reads Bambu Lab spool tags (MIFARE Classic) and NTAG213, NTAG215 and NTAG216 tags, and shows what the tag itself says without a phone: the material of a Bambu Lab tag, the tag type, the UID and how much of the tag was read.
- With the FliBuddy app for iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch over Bluetooth: looks each spool up in Bambuddy, records its weight (gross or net), saves new Bambu Lab spools, links tags to spools, marks spools as new or empty with an undo, and deletes spools.
- Recent lists the spools weighed in this session.
- Get app shows a QR code of the project page with the App Store link.
- Settings for the connection, Auto-save new, After scan, Weigh as, LED and vibration feedback, and sound.
