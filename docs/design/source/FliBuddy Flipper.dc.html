Flipper Zero

A 128×64 screen, 1 bit, six buttons. The rule: one spool, one press. OK always does the main action of the screen, ◀ opens the menu or cancels, ▶ is the quick second action, Back goes back. Every event is confirmed by the LED and the vibro, so there is no need to look at the screen.
